Who am I?

my name is asteria, it's nice to meet you. i've been on the internet for all of my life, and miss the days of yore when the kind places here were kinder, the people you interacted with friendlier, the desire for connection more genuine. i've built this place to reclaim some of that feeling, as our world heats up, the datacenters expand, and society becomes ever atomized. here there are no algorithms, no advertising, no interests being served beyond my own desire to create and share. i hope you enjoy what you find here, and that it might inspire you to reclaim space for yourself.
